Rented Pretty

By Jonathan Grisham

About the Book

He wasn't gay. He was rented. There was a difference, and it was the only thing he had left.

Cal is twenty-nine, still pretty, and already spent. The sickness runs the show now, the money's gone before his shoes are on, and three blocks from the bar there's a room where a man named Orville is always waiting. Always paid. Always patient.

Every time, Cal swears he's done. Every time, he means it. And meaning it has never mattered less.

Grisham does not look away. He does not explain Cal to the reader or ask the reader to forgive him. He follows the economy of a body running out of things to sell and writes it with the same directness he brings to everything: no sentimentality, no redemption arc installed to make the reader feel better, no exit that wasn't there in the first place.

Rented Pretty is for adult readers. It is not a book about recovery. It is a book about what happens inside a life when there is no recovery in sight and you are still, somehow, alive in it.
