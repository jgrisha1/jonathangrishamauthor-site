Where to Start

Reading Order

Start For Appalachian body horror, begin with Kept Warm for the Mountain. For nonfiction religious trauma, begin with Growing Up Independent Baptist. Either tells you who you are dealing with.
Then SPUR for Southern Gothic family horror, Angels for occult dread, The Devil Finds a Home for the debut short story that runs through everything after it.
Last My Baby and The Big Orange Monster Fucked Up are maximum severity. Do not start here. Read the content warnings on each page first.
