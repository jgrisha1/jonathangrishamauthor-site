Short Bio

Jonathan Grisham is an extreme horror and splatterpunk author based in East Tennessee. His fiction has appeared in Terror Tract alongside Edward Lee and Ray Garton, and in Deep Fried Horror: Father's Day Edition. His memoir Growing Up Independent Baptist is a first-person account of religious abuse inside the Independent Fundamental Baptist movement. He has published eight books.

He is a queer writer, a lay leader in the United Methodist Church, and an active member of New Life UMC in Knoxville. He also records and releases music under the name Rust and Redemption. His debut album Deconstruction Roots was released August 16, 2025.

He lives outside Knoxville with his husband David, six dogs, six cats, and chickens.
